## NDVI Mapping of Chittagong District

A remote sensing project focused on assessing the **spatial distribution and vegetation conditions of Chittagong District** using the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI).

Satellite imagery was processed to calculate NDVI and classify vegetation into five levels, ranging from **Very Low to Very High**. The resulting map highlights spatial variations in vegetation cover and demonstrates the application of remote sensing and GIS techniques for environmental assessment.

The project involved **satellite data processing, spectral-index calculation, spatial classification, and thematic map design** using GIS.
