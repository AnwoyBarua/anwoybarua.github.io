<div style="text-align: justify;">
I am a Statistics graduate from the University of Chittagong, Bangladesh, where I completed both my B.Sc. and M.S. in Statistics. I am currently working as a part-time <strong>Research Assistant</strong> under the supervision of Associate Professor Sanjib Ghosh. <br> <br>
My research interests lie at the intersection of <strong>climate science, statistics, and data science</strong>, with a particular focus on climate extremes, climate risk assessment, climate model evaluation, bias correction, and uncertainty quantification. I am also interested in understanding the impacts of climate change on public health.<br> <br>

My technical expertise includes <strong>statistical modeling, machine learning, geospatial analysis, and environmental data analysis</strong>, primarily using Python, R, and GIS tools. I aim to apply my quantitative skills to better understand climate variability, assess climate-related risks, and support evidence-based decision-making.
