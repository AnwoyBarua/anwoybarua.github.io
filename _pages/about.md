I am a Statistics graduate from the University of Chittagong, Bangladesh, where I completed both my B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Statistics. I am currently working as a part-time Research Assistant under the supervision of Associate Professor Sanjib Ghosh. My research interests lie at the intersection of **climate science, statistics, and data science**, with a particular focus on climate extremes, climate risk assessment, climate model evaluation, bias correction, and uncertainty quantification. I am also interested in understanding the impacts of climate change on public health.

My technical expertise includes **statistical modeling, machine learning, geospatial analysis, and environmental data analysis**, primarily using Python, R, and GIS tools. I aim to apply quantitative and computational approaches to better understand climate variability, assess climate-related risks, and support evidence-based decision-making.
