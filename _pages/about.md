<div style="text-align: justify;">
I am a Statistics graduate from the University of Chittagong, Bangladesh, where I completed both my B.Sc. and M.S. in Statistics. I am currently working as a part-time <strong>Research Assistant</strong> under the supervision of Associate Professor Sanjib Ghosh. <br> <br>
  
My research interests lie at the intersection of <strong>atmospheric science, climate science, statistics, and data science</strong>. I am particularly interested in air quality, climate–atmosphere interactions, climate modelling, satellite remote sensing, and the application of statistical and machine learning methods to environmental research. I am also interested in understanding how atmospheric processes and climate change influence air pollution and human health. <br> <br>

My technical expertise includes <strong>statistical modelling, machine learning, geospatial analysis, and environmental data analysis</strong>, primarily using Python, R, and GIS tools. I aim to apply my quantitative background to investigate atmospheric and environmental processes, analyse climate and air quality data, and contribute to a better understanding of environmental challenges.
